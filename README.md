## About Me

I build applied AI systems that run where the data lives: on premise, on local GPUs, with no cloud dependency. My work sits between **document AI**, **computer vision**, **multimodal LLMs** and **ML for security**, with a focus on making models dependable on real world data.

- **MSc in Computer Science and Information Engineering**, National Yunlin University of Science and Technology (YunTech), Taiwan. Top 5% of cohort.

## What I Work On

### Offline Document Intelligence
Building private, fully offline pipelines that turn complex documents into clean, structured data. The work brings together OCR engines, document layout analysis and vision language models, evaluated side by side to find the right balance of accuracy, speed and hardware cost for enterprise use.

**Document types:** PDF, scanned images, Word, PowerPoint, HTML
